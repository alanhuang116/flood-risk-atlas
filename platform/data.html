ZIP-level flood damage estimates for US residential buildings: fluvial, pluvial and coastal flooding, present climate and SSP2-4.5 / SSP5-8.5 at 2030 and 2060.

Release

Data dictionary

One row per ZIP code × scenario-year × flood type. Ratios are value-weighted (sum of losses ÷ sum of full replacement value of structures and contents).
